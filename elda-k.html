How to change this page

  1. Sign in to GitHub with your own account.
  2. In the search box at the top, type emas-solutionmanagement and press Enter. Click furyknl/emas-solutionmanagement in the results.
  3. Click your own file: elda-k.html.
  4. Click the pencil.
  5. GitHub asks you to make your own copy. Click Fork this repository. Expect this message and ignore it: "You need to fork this repository to propose changes. Sorry, you're not able to edit this repository directly…"
  6. Replace "This is my page. I have not changed it yet." with a sentence of your own.
  7. Click Commit changes, write a short message describing the change for future reference, then click Propose changes.
  8. Click Create pull request. That click sends your request to the lecturer.

Your change goes live after the lecturer accepts it.
